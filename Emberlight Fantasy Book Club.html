One fantasy book a month. One quest to finish it.

Emberlight is a monthly book club for fantasy readers. Each month brings one hand-picked book, a weekly discussion, and a themed challenge to try while you read.

How it works

Get the pick

On the first of the month, we announce one fantasy book, with a short note on why it’s worth your time.

Talk it through

Weekly discussion threads are spoiler-safe until the schedule says otherwise. Read at your own pace.

Take the quest

Every month has a small themed challenge, such as drawing a map or writing a scene from a side character’s view.

A look at month one

Theme: found family

Our first pick is announced to the waitlist before anyone else. Founding members get the first month free.

Quest: describe your own party in three lines. Who is the healer, the thief, and the one who keeps everyone fed?

Emberlight is a pilot project. We store your email only to send club updates, and never share it.
